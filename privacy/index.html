1. Overview

Malynda Chuks-Ugwoke ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SunScream mobile application as a free commercial product. This service is provided by us at no out-of-pocket cost and is intended for use as-is. This page informs application users regarding our exact structural rules and compliance parameters collecting, utilizing, and disclosing personal data profiles.

2. Information Collection and Usage Requirements

To provide continuous, automated real-time ultraviolet monitoring, the application requires specific context permissions. The data processed is segmented below:

A. Real-Time Geolocation Data

The application requires background/foreground device location coordinates to poll satellite atmospheric sensors. This geographical information is processed purely on-device and forwarded anonymously to our external meteorological framework (OpenUV API) to pull accurate hazard indices. **We do not save or log your location tracking history on any external proprietary servers.**

B. Device Identification Logs

Standard device log information (such as operating system versions, individual internal hardware configurations, and performance crash intervals) may be processed temporarily by third-party tracking kits to guarantee application software stability updates.

3. App Data Policy & Data Safety Mandate

In strict accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global Data Safety standards, we maintain complete transparency regarding data handling transparency metrics:

Data Encryption

All analytical logs or system data handling generated transiently by the app are transmitted strictly via encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS protocols) during transit to prevent unauthorized intercept routines.

Data Deletion Rights

Since SunScream processes location context temporarily and doesn't manage account setups on external databases, no user profiling traits are stored. You can request absolute data wipe verification by emailing our helpdesk.

5. Tracking and Cookies

Cookies are files containing miniature datasets commonly deployed as anonymous identification trackers. SunScream does not configure or initialize native browser cookies explicitly. However, third-party code elements or mobile advertising software development kits (SDKs) may inject systemic operational tokens to optimize contextual ad layout spaces.

6. Child Data Protection Mandate

Our operational features do not directly address or target audiences under the age of 13. We do not knowingly compile or interact with personal traits associated with children. In any event where discovery metrics indicate a child under 13 has bypassed validation checkpoints and left data trails, we execute instant wipe scripts across all volatile tracking channels.
